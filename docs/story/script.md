# Descender: story and script

First draft, written against the open story issues (#16 names, #17 outline,
#18 delivery, #21 the reveal, #30 NPCs). Everything in sections 1 to 4 is a
proposal; sections 5 to 7 are the script itself. Section 5 (Heaven 1) is in
the game. The rest is written so the later levels can be built against it.

The game is a side-view metroidvania: a man with half-finished angel powers
descends from Heaven, through Purgatory and the ruined Earth, down the nine
circles of Hell to find his brother.

---

## 1. Decisions this draft makes

### Names (#16)

Three pairs that sound like brothers and fit a dark, religious setting that is
still more alive than Blasphemous:

| Hero | Brother | Feel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ansel** | **Tobiah** | Old Testament brother, quiet and plain |
| Caspar | Elian | Softer, more fairy-tale |
| Mattias | Rook | Harder, more soldierly |

This draft uses **Ansel** (the hero) and **Tobiah** (the brother). Tobiah
keeps his human name in Hell. The demons never use it: to them he is **the
Lowest**, because he is the only one who fell all the way to the bottom and
stood up. Keeping the name is what makes the reveal land: the player hears
"the Lowest" for four circles, then one demon says "Tobiah".

### How the story is told (#18)

- **Text dialogue boxes**, no portraits in the first pass (a name tag and
  text). Portraits can be added later without changing a line.
- **Short NPC lines.** Nobody gives a speech. One to three boxes per answer.
- **Item descriptions.** Relics the hero picks up. One or two sentences each,
  and each one tells the player one true thing about the world.
- **Environment**: murals, corpses, the hole in the sky over Earth. Written
  here as stage notes for the level issues.
- **The hero speaks**, in short lines. He asks questions, and he lies to
  himself. He never explains the plot.
- **No cutscenes** in the first milestone. The opening (Tobiah overhears, the
  judgement, the theft) is not shown: the game starts after it, and the
  player reconstructs it from the first level's dialogue and relics.
- **Budget** for the whole game: about 16 conversations, 10 one-line
  triggers, 45 item descriptions, 6 boss intros. Roughly 220 boxes of text.
  Each box fits three short lines.

### The reveal (#21)

The hero learns that his brother is the Lowest in **circle 5**, from a demon
captain who served under him (Gorrow), after beating him. Rescue becomes a
hope that the player can feel is thin. Three hints come earlier:

1. The Lowest's banner is a **tally mark**, the same mark on Tobiah's
   harvest token that the hero carries from the first level.
2. Imps in circles 1 and 2 say the Lowest "talks to someone who isn't
   there" and "says a name, over and over."
3. A message scratched into the wall in circle 3: `KEEP COUNT. DON'T
   FOLLOW.` next to a tally. Tobiah told Ansel to keep count when they were
   boys.

## 2. The truth of the world

What the player pieces together, in the order they learn it:

1. **Heaven is a garden.** The good dead live there, tended by angels, under
   a sun that never sets. (Known at the start.)
2. **The garden is a farm.** The blessed are "gathered": taken to the Basin,
   wiped, and made into angels. Angels are made of people. (Heaven 1 and 2.)
3. **The Wheel is a lie.** The dead are promised rebirth. Nobody is reborn.
   The Wheel is the Basin. (Heaven 2, confirmed in circles 6 and 7.)
4. **Those who refuse, or who find out, are sent down** through the Low
   Gate. Hell is where the farm throws its refuse. (Purgatory.)
5. **Demons are the sent-down**, worn down to teeth by the fall. The war on
   Earth is a prison riot climbing up. (Earth.)
6. **Someone organised the riot.** The Lowest. (Circles 1 to 4.)
7. **The Lowest is Tobiah.** (Circle 5.)
8. **God is the first angel.** The Gardener, who no longer remembers being a
   person and keeps itself alive on the harvest because the Making is the
   only thing it still knows how to do. (The clock ending.)

### What happened before the game starts

Ansel and Tobiah died young, in the same winter, and were judged good. They
spent what felt like years in the Garden. Tobiah was curious and talked to
everyone, including the angel guards at the Low Gate. One day he overheard
two Wardens speak of "the pressing" and the quota, followed a gathering, and
saw the Basin. He told Ansel. He also told others; that was the mistake.

The Choir judged Tobiah "ungathered": not fit to be made, not fit to stay.
Ansel watched his brother walked to the Low Gate and dropped. The Wardens let
him watch; it is meant to be a lesson.

Ansel broke into the Making-house that same endless day. He meant to be
made: wings to come back up, light to fight with. He skipped the Basin, which
is the first step, and started at the second seal. The Makers caught him
halfway. He tore free and ran into the outskirts with one wing's worth of
light in him and a Choir voice in his head telling him to come back and be
finished.

That is where the game begins.

## 4. Beat outline (#17)

One numbered list, each beat tied to its area and to how it is delivered
(NPC, item, trigger, boss, environment).

### Heaven 1, the outskirts (prototype level, in the game)

1. **Coming to.** Trigger at the start. Ansel wakes half-made. The Voice
   tells him to return to the Basin. He refuses. (trigger `opening`)
2. **Mereth.** NPC in the orchard. She saw Tobiah walked to the Low Gate,
   "talking the whole way". She tells Ansel the gate is the way down and
   gives him the direction. (NPC)
3. **The harvest token.** Item. Tobiah's token with his tally scratched
   in. The player carries it for the whole game. (item)
4. **The feather.** The wings pickup. Existing ability message. (pickup)
5. **The ledger page.** Item. The quota: "Gathered this season: 40.
   Short: 3." (item)
6. **Ithuriel.** NPC by the checkpoint. A guard who sat down. He knows a
   song he should not know; Tobiah used to hum it. The wipe is not clean.
   (NPC, two conversations)
7. **The basin shard.** Item, near the gate. A piece of the Basin. (item)
8. **The Low Gate's outer door.** The goal gate. Trigger line as Ansel
   reaches it: the Voice, louder. (trigger `gate`)

### Heaven 2, the terraces and the Low Gate

9. **The terraces.** Environment: gathered humans walking in lines, calm.
   Guards. A mural of the Wheel. Items: a child's tally stone, a Warden's
   prayer card ("What is pressed is not lost"), a Maker's glove.
10. **The Making-house, seen from outside.** Trigger. The Voice: "You left
    your other half on the table." Ansel: "Keep it."
11. **The Warden of the Low Gate.** Boss. The angel who dropped Tobiah. It
    does not remember doing so. Intro and defeat lines. Dropping through the
    Low Gate ends the level.

### Purgatory

12. **The shaft.** Environment: a fall that goes on. Names scratched in the
    walls by people falling. One in Tobiah's hand: `KEEP COUNT.`
13. **The Ferryman.** NPC. Refused the Basin, refused to fall. Explains:
    "Down there, the fall takes something each circle. By the bottom, teeth."
    Trades. (NPC, three conversations across visits)
14. **The light dash feather.** Here in the design; in Heaven 1 in the
    prototype. (pickup)
15. **The last door.** Trigger before Earth: a hole in the floor of
    Purgatory, and below it, sky.

### Earth, the hub

16. **The hole in the sky.** Environment: the town under a torn sky, demons
    climbing out of the ground, angels coming down to "gather" whoever is
    left. Humans caught between.
17. **Survivors.** NPCs: Old Pell (shop), Hanne (draws the banner), Corporal
    Dace (gives the clock face, starts the side quest), Sister Ava (heard
    the name). Each has a first conversation and later ones that change as
    the hero brings news from below.
18. **Side effects of the war (#24).** Items: a gathering warrant pinned to a
    church door, a demon's climbing hook, a soldier's letter.

### Circles 1 and 2 (the Shallows, the Winds)

19. **Fresh fallen.** Imps still talk. They say the Lowest promised them
    "up". (enemy lines, item: an imp's tally bone)
20. **Gorrow, first meeting.** NPC/boss encounter. He lets Ansel pass, on
    orders: "He said a half-angel would come. He said not to kill you."
21. **The light bolt.** Ability. Item description for its source.

### Circles 3 and 4 (the Mire, the Furnace)

22. **`KEEP COUNT. DON'T FOLLOW.`** Environment, with a tally. Ansel's line.
23. **Vael the Bargainer.** NPC. Buys memories. Offers to buy the token.
    "Someone down below is paying well for anything with that mark."
24. **The glide.** Ability.

### Circle 5 (the Seal)

25. **Gorrow, the fight.** Boss. After it, the reveal: he says the name.
    Ansel's goal changes from rescue to "then I will bring him home", which
    the player has just learned is not a kind thing to want. (boss + NPC)
26. **The seal break.** Ability. The seal is Choir-made: Heaven built the
    walls of Hell.

### Circles 6 and 7 (the Glass, the Archive)

27. **True sight.** Ability. Shows the hidden: the Basin's pipes run down
    here. Hell is plumbed into Heaven. The Wheel is a lie, in writing.
28. **Tobiah's messengers.** NPC: a sent-down angel whose mouth he uses.
    "Go home, Ansel." Ansel: "You first."
29. **Vael, second visit.** Explains what the Lowest is doing: climbing with
    an army to break the Basin. "He is not wrong, you know."
30. **Clock pieces.** Three of the five are here if the player looks.

### Circles 8 and 9 (the Gate Below, the Floor)

31. **The last camp.** Environment: the demons' army, resting. They let
    Ansel through. They know who he is.
32. **The brothers.** Trigger and boss intro. The only long conversation in
    the game, six boxes each. Then the fight.
33. **Main ending.** Ansel wins. Tobiah will not go up. What Ansel does is
    the player's last choice in the dialogue: carry him (Tobiah dies on the
    way, ending A1), or sit down with him at the bottom (ending A2). Both
    are the "main" ending; the game does not say which is right.

### The clock ending

34. **Five pieces.** The clock face (Dace), the hand (Ferryman), the weight
    (circle 6), the chain (circle 7), the key (Vael, for the token). Assembled
    at the Archive in circle 7, where the pipes come down.
35. **Back up the pipes.** The clock runs backward. Ansel climbs to the
    Garden before the game: the day before Tobiah overheard. He can hear
    Tobiah, alive, in the orchard. He does not go to him. He goes to the
    Making-house.
36. **The Gardener.** Final conversation and fight. God is the first angel,
    the only one never wiped because there was no one to do it, and it has
    forgotten everything but the Making. It farms because it is hungry and
    because it does not know what else a heaven is for.
37. **Ending B.** Ansel kills the Gardener. The sun sets in Heaven for the
    first time. The Making stops. The harvest stops. Tobiah will never
    overhear anything, because there is nothing to overhear. The brothers
    stay in the Garden, which is just a garden now. Ansel keeps the token.
    Tobiah asks what the tally is for. Ansel: "Days. Start at one."

## 5. Script: Heaven 1, the outskirts (in the game)

Format: `ID` is the key in `dialogue/world_1.json`. Each line is one box.
Three short lines fit a box; keep a box under about 140 characters.

Speaker tags: **ANSEL**, **VOICE** (the Choir in his head), **MERETH**,
**ITHURIEL**. Item descriptions have no speaker.

### `opening` (trigger, once, at the start)

> **VOICE:** You are unfinished.
> **VOICE:** Return to the Basin. Lie down. We will take the rest.
> **ANSEL:** No.
> **VOICE:** Then it will hurt. Everything you carry will hurt.
> **ANSEL:** Good. Then I'll know I'm still carrying it.

Sets flag `opening_seen`.

### `mereth_intro` (Mereth, first talk)

> **MERETH:** Hush. Don't stand so straight, they'll see the light in you.
> **MERETH:** You're the other one. The brother. I saw you run.
> **ANSEL:** Where did they take him?
> **MERETH:** Down. The Low Gate, past the orchard, past the guards. He went talking.
> **MERETH:** The whole way. Telling the guards what he saw. He wanted them to hear it.
> **ANSEL:** That's Tobiah.
> **MERETH:** The gate opens for the gathered and for the dropped. You're neither. You'll have to break it.

Sets flag `met_mereth`.

### `mereth_again`, `mereth_count`, `mereth_orchard` (Mereth, repeat lines, in turn)

> **MERETH:** Keep count. The sun never sets here; it's the only way to know how long it's been.
> **MERETH:** I've been gathered twice. They put me back both times. "Not ripe." I don't ask what that means.

> **MERETH:** Three thousand and twelve. Don't ask me what of. Suns, I suppose. Mine.

> **MERETH:** The orchard's full again. It fills up the day after a gathering. Nobody plants it.

### `ithuriel_intro` (Ithuriel, first talk)

> **ITHURIEL:** Stop there. I am... I am meant to stop you.
> **ITHURIEL:** I sat down an hour ago. I can't remember why. Or how long an hour is.
> **ANSEL:** You're a guard. Guard.
> **ITHURIEL:** There's a song. I keep humming it. I don't know a single song. Angels don't.
> **ANSEL:** Hum it.
> *(Ithuriel hums. Ansel knows it.)*
> **ANSEL:** Where did you hear that?
> **ITHURIEL:** From the one they dropped. He hummed it at the gate. I think... I think I knew it before that.

Sets flag `met_ithuriel`.

### `ithuriel_again`, `ithuriel_song`, `ithuriel_orders` (Ithuriel, repeat lines, in turn)

> **ITHURIEL:** They say the Basin takes everything. It doesn't. Something stays in the hands.
> **ITHURIEL:** Go on. I'll say I didn't see you. I'm good at not remembering.

> **ITHURIEL:** (He hums. He stops when he notices you.) Still don't know it. Still can't stop.

> **ITHURIEL:** My orders say 'none pass'. They don't say I have to stand up.

### `gate` (trigger, once, at the goal gate)

> **VOICE:** This is the Low Gate. Nothing that goes through it comes back up.
> **ANSEL:** He will.
> **VOICE:** The Garden will go on without you both. It always has.

Sets flag `reached_gate`.

### Items in Heaven 1

**Tobiah's harvest token** (`token`)
> A wooden harvest token, the kind the gathered carry to the Basin. Someone
> has scratched a tally into the back: four strokes and a bar, over and over,
> until the wood ran out.

**Ledger page** (`ledger`)
> Torn from the Wardens' book. "Gathered this season: forty. Short of the
> Choir's need by three. Ungathered: one, dropped. Note the brother."

**Basin shard** (`basin_shard`)
> A curved piece of white stone, smooth as a bowl. It is warm, and when you
> hold it you cannot remember what you were thinking a moment ago.

Each item sets flag `item:<id>` and stays collected in the save.

### Existing messages kept

The feather and light-dash pickups keep their ability messages. The death
message ("The angels' system pulls you back...") fits: the Making brings him
back to the last checkpoint.

## 6. Script: Earth, Hollowmere (in the game)

The hub under the hole in the sky: the Ruined Earth region of the world,
with its lines in `dialogue/world_1.json`. Four survivors, three relics, a trigger on
arrival and one at the churchyard hole that leads down.

Every NPC has unique conversations that play once each, in order; some
wait for a flag (a relic the hero has read, or `reveal` from Circle 5).
After those, the NPC rotates through its repeat lines, one per talk, and
goes back to a unique line the moment a new one unlocks. Dace hands over
the clock face in talk (flag `clock_face`) rather than as a relic, so it
cannot be missed on the floor.

### `arrival` (once; sets `arrived_earth`)

> **ANSEL:** Sky. Under me, then over me. So that is what the floor of Purgatory is.
> *(A town under a torn sky. Fires. Something moving in the churchyard, and someone hammering.)*
> **ANSEL:** Someone is still here.

### `pell_intro` (once; sets `met_pell`)

> **OLD PELL:** Don't stand in the light, lad. They see the light. ...You came down out of it, didn't you.
> **OLD PELL:** One hand. A brute took the other. I can still hammer; I can't hold the tongs. You hold, I hit. Deal?
> **ANSEL:** What are we making?
> **OLD PELL:** Something that goes down and comes back. Nobody's managed the second part yet.

### `pell_sky` (once; needs `met_pell`)

> **OLD PELL:** The sky opened a year ago. First the demons came up out of the churchyard. Then the angels came down. We thought that was help.
> **OLD PELL:** It wasn't help. They were gathering. Same as the demons, only cleaner about it.
> **ANSEL:** I was one of them. Half of one.
> **OLD PELL:** I know. I've seen the hands. Yours haven't learned to hold yet.

### `pell_hook` (once; needs `item:hook`)

> **OLD PELL:** That hook. Give it here.
> **OLD PELL:** Thigh bone and a hinge. That hinge is off the church door. I hung that door.
> **OLD PELL:** They're not animals, whatever Dace says. Animals don't use hinges.

### `pell_warrant` (once; needs `item:warrant`)

> **OLD PELL:** Forty names on that paper. My wife's is the ninth. 'Called to the Garden early.'
> **OLD PELL:** When you get back up there, you tell me what garden.

### `pell_forge_first` (once; needs `met_pell`; sets `sword:1`)

> **OLD PELL:** Church iron. My own stock, and the last of it. Hold the blade on the horn. No, like that. Steadier.
> **OLD PELL:** There. It'll go through a brute's hide now, which is more than mine did.
> **ANSEL:** What do I owe you?
> **OLD PELL:** More of it, if the ground has any. They hang their doors with it down there. Bring me a bar and I'll hammer it in.

### `pell_iron` (once; needs `sword:1` and the Heresy iron; sets `sword:2`)

> **OLD PELL:** That's church iron. Where did you... no. Don't tell me. Give it here.
> **OLD PELL:** Hold. Hit. Hold. Hit. ...That's as hard as I can make it, lad. The rest is you.

### `pell_forge`

> **OLD PELL:** Hold it steady. Steadier. Right, now don't move.
> **OLD PELL:** Good. That's one more they'll have to get through.

### `pell_light`

> **OLD PELL:** Keep your light down when you go in the ground. Both sides can see it.

### `pell_boy`

> **OLD PELL:** I've a boy about your age. Had. Go on, I'm busy.

### `hanne_intro` (once; sets `met_hanne`)

> **HANNE:** You're not from the sky. The sky ones don't jump, they just come down.
> **HANNE:** I'm Hanne. I draw what I see so I don't have to keep seeing it.
> **HANNE:** Want to see the big one's flag? Come back and I'll show you.

### `hanne_drawing` (once; needs `met_hanne`; sets `hanne_drawing`)

> **HANNE:** Here. It's not a picture. It's just lines.
> *(A tally mark. Four strokes and a fifth across.)*
> **ANSEL:** Where did you see this?
> **HANNE:** On the ones that climb. They cut it into themselves.
> **HANNE:** They say the Lowest says it means 'I'm still counting'.

### `hanne_token` (once; needs `hanne_drawing`, `item:token`)

> **HANNE:** That's the same lines. On your wooden coin. Did you cut them in yourself?
> **ANSEL:** No. Someone counted me.
> **HANNE:** Then he's down there. The one who counts. Say hello from the drawing girl.

### `hanne_churchyard`

> **HANNE:** Don't go near the churchyard. That's where they come up. I drew it. It's a bad one.

### `hanne_draw_down`

> **HANNE:** Pell says you're going down. Will you look at everything? I can't see down from here.

### `hanne_count`

> **HANNE:** I counted the angels this morning. Nine. Yesterday it was eleven. Where do they go?

### `dace_intro` (once; sets `met_dace`)

> **CORPORAL DACE:** Halt. ...Halt doesn't work on your kind, does it. Fine.
> **CORPORAL DACE:** Corporal Dace, Third Company. What's left of it.
> **ANSEL:** Where's the rest?
> **CORPORAL DACE:** In the ground. Some of them walking about in it.

### `dace_clock` (once; needs `met_dace`; sets `clock_face`)

> **CORPORAL DACE:** Clock face. Church tower. Found it in a demon's pack. Don't know why it wanted it.
> **CORPORAL DACE:** It's the only thing I've seen one of them carry that it didn't eat. Take it. Find out.
> *(A brass clock face with no hands. Four shapes are punched around the rim, where the hands would be pinned.)*

### `dace_letter` (once; needs `item:letter`)

> **CORPORAL DACE:** You read Thorne's letter. He never sent it. I kept meaning to.
> **CORPORAL DACE:** He's down there now. If you meet a lad in a red scarf, he was mine. Don't tell him I said so.

### `dace_barricade`

> **CORPORAL DACE:** Barricade's holding. Barricade's always holding, until it isn't.

### `dace_waiting`

> **CORPORAL DACE:** They don't rush. They climb a bit, look at us, climb back. Like they're waiting for an order.

### `dace_ticking`

> **CORPORAL DACE:** Clock's still ticking somewhere. I hear it at night. Nobody else does.

### `ava_intro` (once; sets `met_ava`)

> **SISTER AVA:** A church is where you go when you want to be heard. I stopped coming for that.
> **SISTER AVA:** I stopped praying when I understood who was listening.
> **ANSEL:** Who?
> **SISTER AVA:** Someone who keeps a garden. And weeds it.

### `ava_name` (once; needs `met_ava`)

> **SISTER AVA:** The demons shout upward when they climb. Not at us. Past us. A name. Every time the same name.
> **ANSEL:** What name?
> **SISTER AVA:** I won't say it in a church.
> **SISTER AVA:** Outside, perhaps. If you come back up.

### `ava_reveal` (once; needs `reveal`)

> **SISTER AVA:** You've heard it now. It's on your face.
> **SISTER AVA:** Then I'll say it, church or no. Tobiah. They shout it like a prayer. Perhaps it is one.

### `ava_hand`

> **SISTER AVA:** The warrant on the door is in the Choir's hand. I used to copy that hand. I taught it.

### `ava_listen`

> **SISTER AVA:** Pell swears, Dace counts, Hanne draws. I listen. Someone has to.

### `ava_below`

> **SISTER AVA:** Go carefully. Below is only above with the lights put out.

### `item_warrant`

> **GATHERING WARRANT:** Nailed to the church door. 'By the Choir's need, the following are called to the Garden early.'
> **GATHERING WARRANT:** Forty names. The ink is fresh.
> **ANSEL:** Called early. Mereth was 'not ripe'. They're harvesting.

### `item_hook`

> **CLIMBING HOOK:** Demon-made, from a thigh bone and a hinge. The hinge is from a church door.

## 7. Script: later areas (drafted, not in the game yet)

These are the beats above as lines. They will change when the levels are
designed, but the voice and the facts should hold.

### Heaven 2

In the game: the `makinghouse` trigger stands on the ledge before the
Warden's room in Paradise, the Warden's lines play as the boss's intro and
defeat (`seraph_warden_intro`, `seraph_warden_defeat`, the latter sets
`warden_fallen`), and the three items are relics on the lower terraces.

**`makinghouse` (trigger, outside the Making-house)**
> **VOICE:** You left your other half on the table in there.
> **ANSEL:** Keep it.
> **VOICE:** We will. We keep everything.

**Warden of the Low Gate, boss intro**
> **WARDEN:** Ungathered. You were seen at the Making. You will be finished.
> **ANSEL:** You dropped my brother.
> **WARDEN:** I have dropped many. I remember none of them. That is a mercy they give us.

**Warden, defeat**
> **WARDEN:** The gate... does not open from this side.
> **ANSEL:** I know. I'm not opening it.
> *(He breaks it. The level ends in the fall.)*

### Purgatory

In the game: the `shaft` trigger at the bottom of the passage from Earth,
and the Ferryman by the middle checkpoint (`ferryman_intro`,
`ferryman_hand` sets `clock_hand`, then three repeat lines).

**`shaft` (trigger, during the fall or on a ledge)**
> **ANSEL:** Names. Hundreds of them. Scratched going down.
> *(One, in a hand he knows: KEEP COUNT.)*
> **ANSEL:** I am.

**Ferryman, first talk**

## 9. How it is built (for the level issues)

- Dialogue for a level lives in `dialogue/<level>.json`: one entry per id
  with `lines` (speaker and text), and optional `requires` / `unless` flag
  lists, `sets` (a flag set when it finishes) and `once` (plays a single
  time; every finished entry sets `seen:<id>`).
- NPCs, relics and triggers are placed in `levels/world/placements.json`:
  each object has a tile cell, a
  type (`npc`, `item`, `trigger`), a sprite, a name, the unique dialogue ids
  to try in order and a `rotate` list of repeat lines. The first unique
  entry whose flags match is the one that plays; when none does, the next
  repeat line in turn plays. So an NPC's lines change as the story moves
  and never run dry.
- NPC sprites are drawn by `tools/npc_sprites/npcs.py` (a small rig: idle
  and talk loops, 64x48 frames) and exported with `export.py` into
  `assets/sprites/<name>/`; the Npc node plays `talk` while its box is open.
- Story flags live in the `Story` autoload and are written to the save with
  everything else. Collected relics are flags (`item:<id>`), so a loaded
  save does not show them again.
- The interact key is `E` (gamepad: Y is heal, so interact is the left
  bumper) and is rebindable in Settings like every other key.
- Issues this draft answers: #16 (names), #17 (outline), #18 (delivery),
  #21 (reveal), and #30 (NPCs) for the hub. It does not decide #13
  (circle names); the circle names above are placeholders.
